Beyond the Science: Discovering the People Behind Cytiva



My First Impression of Industry 

Stepping into a professional environment for the first time, I was unsure what to expect as a teenager. I pictured bleak, industrial rooms and difficult to approach employees however the reality was quite the opposite. My first conversation with someone from HR created an immediate sense of openness and belonging. His curiosity and willingness to work at my pace quickly proved me wrong as I was warmly welcomed to the office. Within my first few moments at Cytiva, I began to realise that the workplace was not defined only by the work being done, but also by the people who created the environment around it. 

Understanding the Bigger Picture Behind a Product 

I was also given the opportunity to tour the factory where I was introduced to the filtering process. Walking into a lab and seeing actual scientists work on something impactful was fascinating to say the least. Seeing fibres get analysed and laminated into filters used in biopharmaceutical manufacturing  helped me understand all the unseen roles needed to bring a product into the world.

As the week progressed, my understanding of what makes a company successful expanded as I realised Cytiva wasn't just scientists working in labs. The initial thought of researchers and chemists broadened to roles from sourcing to intellectual property and I realised how within one company, there are many different career paths and roles that connect. Areas that I initially overlooked were actually the heart of keeping the business together which made me realise that many skills can transfer across industries, creating more possibilities that I had previously considered. Customer management particularly stood out to me, as it was an area I had never previously considered but plays an essential role in connecting the company with the people it serves. I quickly learned that companies are built around the people and systems supporting the product rather than just the product itself.

Lessons That Extend Beyond the Workplace 

During my conversations with different teams, one of the most valuable parts of the experience was hearing advice from members of the legal team. Over the years, I had heard successful people talk about discipline, effort and growth before–but usually from a distance. Hearing the same ideas from someone who had built a career through them and was willing to share them with someone at the stage where important decisions are being made was not only inspirational, but also eye-opening. Discussing consistency, the Kaizen principle of being 1% better everyday and surrounding yourself with people who help you grow helped me reflect on my own ambitions and the person I aspire to become. I also found the discussion around pressure particularly valuable, as it highlighted the importance of using challenges as motivation while recognising the need for balance. It reminded me that success is not one huge achievement, but the result of effort, discipline, and the people you choose to surround yourself with every day.

A New Perspective on Work

Before Cytiva, I viewed work mainly as a responsibility: something people did to provide for themselves. However, seeing people who were genuinely passionate about their roles changed this perspective. I realised that a workplace can also be a place to grow, build relationships and continue learning. In just three days, I went from feeling uncertain about the world of work to leaving with a greater curiosity about the opportunities ahead and the confidence to continue exploring them.
